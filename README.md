# HR Analytics: Employee Hierarchy & Compensation Equity

## Project Overview
This project utilizes advanced SQL to analyze a corporate HR dataset. The objective was to map the organizational hierarchy, identify pay inequities relative to performance, and flag high-performing employees at risk of turnover (flight risks).

## The Business Problem
The executive team needed to understand if compensation and promotion structures were aligned with actual performance metrics, and whether the company was at risk of losing top talent due to lack of career progression.

## SQL Techniques Demonstrated
1.  **Recursive CTEs:** Used to map the organizational hierarchy (identifying the chain of command from the CEO down to entry-level employees).
2.  **Window Functions:** Applied to calculate the average salary across different role levels to identify overpaid/underperforming employees.
3.  **Conditional Aggregation (CASE):** Created flags for "Flight Risk" (High performers who have not been promoted in 3+ years).
